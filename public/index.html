How it works

From counter to customer
in three moves.

The tag is a tiny URL. The phone is the card reader. Everything else happens in Taployal.

01

Stick a tag on the counter

Register any NFC sticker in seconds from the dashboard. Each one carries your unique earn link — waterproof, battery-free, always on.

02

Customers tap & earn

A phone tap opens your branded earn page. First-timers join with a name and email; regulars just watch their balance climb.

03

Rewards bring them back

Points convert to perks right from the customer's wallet. You see every tap, tier and redemption in real time.
